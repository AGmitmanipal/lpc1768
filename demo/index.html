This browser demo generates its own signal. It does not connect to your board. The delivered firmware draws waveforms and FFT spectra directly on an ILI9341 screen.

What this demonstrates

The capture retains 250 samples before the trigger and 749 after it. AUTO forces a capture after a timeout. NORMAL waits for a crossing. SINGLE holds after one triggered capture. The voltage scale is the ADC pin voltage. Input offset is fixed at 1.65 V.

Square and triangle waves contain harmonics. At low sampling rates those harmonics can alias even when their fundamental is below Nyquist. A real instrument needs analogue filtering and calibration. The FFT mode displays the 256-point radix-2 Cooley-Tukey spectrum computed directly on the capture frame with Bartlett windowing.
